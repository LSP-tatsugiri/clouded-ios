How it works

  1. Capture

    Type it in. No format, no fields, half-formed is fine.

  2. Extraction finds the hard part

    It lists what the idea takes as skills you can answer yes or no to, and marks the one most likely to kill the project.

  3. Your distance from buildable

    Rate what you can do, once. Every idea then says how far it is from you, and which single skill would move the most ideas.
