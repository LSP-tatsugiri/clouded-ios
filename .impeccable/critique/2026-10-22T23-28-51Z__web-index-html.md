## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: product-specific vocabulary on generic SaaS chrome. The bracket marks in Plex Mono (status language and rating control at once), the red crux band "the hard part", the split-flap crux reveal, the indeterminate bar that refuses to estimate time, and cost at the point of action are authored for this product. The 18px white cards on pale blue-grey, the #3461e6 button, pill tabs and sticky side panel are category-interchangeable.

Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect web/` exit 2, 10 findings — side-tab 4 (2 real rules: style.css:411 `.crux`, :859 `.capdetail.is-crux`; the rest rendered copies), low-contrast 3 (white on dark-mode --flow #5b84ff = 3.4:1, real, dark mode only), flat-type-hierarchy 1 (advisory, steps of 1.15), marquee 1 (false positive: the progress sweep, off under reduced motion), bounce-easing 1 (style.css:683, real, minor). Browser overlay on landing + sign-in: 7 findings — side-tab, nested-cards (borderline, mock card), two stragglers ("fine.", "swings"), text-overflow on flap tiles (false positive, deliberate half-glyph clipping), bounce-easing. Side-tab is the crux band, the product's signature: a deliberate design call, not slop. The detector caught the dark-mode button contrast that the design review missed.

## Minor Observations

- Dark-mode filled buttons fail contrast: white on #5b84ff is 3.4:1 (style.css:24, 234). Darken dark --flow for button fills or use dark text.
- Bounce easing on button hover (style.css:683).
- Stragglers on the landing ("fine.", "swings"): text-wrap: pretty.
- "N of M shown" excludes vague ideas that Newest still shows.
- The ▣ picture tag has no accessible text.
- Two h1s on Profile and Group (brand + page head).
- The landing ends with no closing CTA; "invite-only" first appears inside the dialog.
